[[requirements-for-assurance-level-1-certificates]]
=== Requirements for assurance level 1 certificates

[[assurance-level-1-objective-1]]
==== O.1 Conforming GP instance provides proof of its eligibility during automated certificate enrollment.

*Requirement:* <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST verify that the entity attempting certificate enrollment is an instance of a <<subscriber,subscriber>>’s generator product, through an authentication method commonly accepted for certificate enrollment (e.g. SCEP, EST, ACME, client certificates, etc.).

[[assurance-level-1-objective-2]]
==== O.2 GP TOE protects the confidentiality of the signing key.

No stipulation.

[[assurance-level-1-objective-3]]
==== O.3 - GP TOE protects the Claim Generator from exploits, misconfiguration and misuse.

No stipulation.

[[assurance-level-1-objective-4]]
==== O.4 - GP TOE protects the asset and assertions from being tampered with at generation.

No stipulation.

[[assurance-level-1-objective-5]]
==== O.5 - GP TOE protects the traffic between subsystems and components of those subsystems from being intercepted and/or modified.

No stipulation.

[[assurance-level-1-objective-6]]
==== O.6 - Hosting environment is protected from exploit, misconfiguration and misuse.

No stipulation.
