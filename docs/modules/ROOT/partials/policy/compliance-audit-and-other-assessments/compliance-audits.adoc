[[compliance-audits]]
=== Compliance audits

[[regular-audits]]
==== Regular audits

. The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST undergo regular compliance audits conducted by an independent, qualified third-party auditor.
. Audits MUST assess the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s adherence to this <<certificate-policy,CP>>, relevant industry standards (e.g., WebTrust for <<certification-authority,CA>>), and any applicable legal or regulatory requirements.
. The audit frequency MUST depend on the risk profile and assurance level, but at a minimum, annual audits are recommended.

[[audit-scope]]
==== Audit scope

Audits SHOULD cover all aspects of the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s operations relevant to this <<certificate-policy,CP>>, with particular emphasis on:

. <<certificate,certificate>> issuance, renewal, and revocation processes;
. <<attestation,attestation>> validation procedures;
. key management practices within devices or applications and the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s infrastructure;
. logical and procedural security controls;
. personnel security and training related to attestation validation and certificate management; and
. incident response and disaster recovery procedures.

[[audit-reports]]
==== Audit reports

. The <<certification-authority,CA>> SHOULD make audit reports available to the CAWG steering committee and other authorized parties upon request.
. Audit reports SHOULD clearly identify any non-conformities or areas for improvement.
. Audit reports SHOULD be in International English or be accompanied by a professional translation to English.
. The <<certification-authority,CA>> SHOULD take corrective action to address any 
identified deficiencies.
