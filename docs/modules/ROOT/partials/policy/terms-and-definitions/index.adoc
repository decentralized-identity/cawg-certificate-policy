[[terms-and-definitions]]
== Terms and definitions

[[administering-authority]]Administering authority::
+
--
The party that the CAWG <<governing-authority,governing authority>> empowers to operate its <<cawg-conformance-program,conformance program>> on its behalf.
It recognizes and accredits key conformance roles which agree to participate in the program.
The CAWG Trust Task Force of the Creator Assertions Working Group operates in this capacity.
--

[[affiliate]]Affiliate::
+
--
A corporation, partnership, joint venture or other entity controlling, controlled by, or under common control with another entity, or an agency, department, political subdivision, or any entity operating under the direct control of a <<government-entity,government entity>>.
--

[[applicant]]Applicant::
+
--
The <<natural-person,natural person>> or <<legal-entity,legal entity>> that applies for (or seeks renewal of) a <<certificate,certificate>>.
Once the <<certificate,certificate>> is issued, the <<applicant,applicant>> is referred to as the <<subscriber,subscriber>>.
For <<certificate,certificates>> issued to devices, the <<applicant,applicant>> is the entity that controls or operates the device named in the <<certificate,certificate>>, even if the device is sending the actual <<certificate-request,certificate request>>.
--

[[applicants-representative]]Applicant’s representative::
+
--
A <<natural-person,natural person>> or human sponsor who is either the <<applicant,applicant>>, employed by the <<applicant,applicant>>, or an authorized agent who has express authority to represent the <<applicant,applicant>>:

. who signs and submits, or approves a <<certificate-request,certificate request>> on behalf of the <<applicant,applicant>>;
. who signs and submits a <<subscriber-agreement,subscriber agreement>> on behalf of the <<applicant,applicant>>; and/or
. who acknowledges the <<terms-of-use,terms of use>> on behalf of the <<applicant,applicant>> when the <<applicant,applicant>> is an <<affiliate,affiliate>> of the <<certification-authority,CA>> or is the <<certification-authority,CA>>.
--

+
--
A supplier of email client software or other relying-party application software such as mail user agents (web-based or application based) and email service providers that process S/MIME <<certificate,certificates>>.

[[assurance-level]]Assurance level::
+
--
[[_CAWG_claim]]
An indication to a <<relying-party,relying party>> of the level of confidence that it may have that identity assertions and claims signed with a given <<claimsigning-certs,CAWG claim signing certificate>> reflect the actual claimant.
A higher <<assurance-level,assurance level>> allows the <<relying-party,relying party>> to have a greater level of confidence.

The <<assurance-level,assurance level>> is conveyed through the `CAWG-al` (`TBD`) X.509 v3 certificate extension in a <<claimsigning-certs,CAWG claim signing certificate>>.
The value of this extension is an encoded <<object-identifier,OID>> value that corresponds to a numeric value no higher than the <<maximum-assurance-level,maximum assurance level>> for a given claim signing issuer.
The <<object-identifier,OID>> values corresponding to each <<assurance-level,assurance level>> are defined in the CAWG `oid.txt` MIB definition file.

The <<assurance-level,assurance level>> in the <<claimsigning-certs,CAWG claim signing certificate>> that is issued to an organization or individual may be lower than the <<maximum-assurance-level,maximum assurance level>> that the <<issuer,issuer>> is potentially eligible for.
--

[[attestation]]Attestation::
+
--
A letter attesting that <<subject-information,subject information>> is correct written by an accountant, lawyer, government official, or other reliable third party customarily relied upon for such information.
--

[[audit-period]]Audit period::
+
--
In a period-of-time audit, the period between the first day (start) and the last day of operations (end) covered by the auditors in their engagement.
(This is not the same as the period of time when the auditors are on-site at the <<certification-authority,CA>>.)
The coverage rules and maximum length of audit periods are defined in <<compliance-audits>>.
--

[[audit-report]]Audit report::
+
--
A report from a <<qualified-auditor,qualified auditor>> stating the <<qualified-auditor,qualified auditor>>’s opinion on whether an entity’s processes and controls comply with the mandatory provisions of these requirements.
--

[[ca-key-pair]]CA key pair::
+
--
A <<key-pair,key pair>> where the <<public-key,public key>> appears as the <<subject-public-key-info,subject public key info>> in one or more <<root-certification-authority-certificate,root CA certificate>>(s) and/or <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CA>> <<certificate,certificate>>(s).
--

[[cawg-claim-signing-certificate]]CAWG claim signing certificate::
+
--
[[_generator_product]]
An X.509 certificate issued by one of the <<certification-authority,certification authorities>> on the <<cawg-trust-list,CAWG trust list>> to an instance of the <<_generator_product,Conforming Implementer>>’s conforming <<_generator_product,generator product>>, and names the <<_generator_product,generator product>> as the subject of the certificate.

====
C2PA leftover: this issues the certificate to a generator product, which is C2PA’s claim-signing model.

The same applies to the CA, Subscriber and Signer definitions.

[[cawg-conformance-program]]CAWG conformance program::
+
--
A risk-based governance program intended to hold <<applicant,applicants>> who want to demonstrate their conformance to its requirements and then differentiate themselves through CAWG recognition by satisfying program requirements being acknowledged as achieving that level of conformance.
It consists of a set of processes, policies, and requirements governing the designation of <<applicant,applicant>> <<certification-authority,certification authorities>> as adhering to the <<cawg-certificate-policy,CAWG certificate policy>>, as defined by the CAWG Trust Task Force.

Processes include:

* Evaluation of the processes, controls, and technical capabilities of <<certification-authority,certification authorities>> as required by the <<cawg-certificate-policy,CAWG certificate policy>>
* Signing of the requisite legal agreements to become a member of the program.
--

[[cawg-governance-framework]]CAWG governance framework::
+
--
A collection of governance documents which defines the CAWG trust ecosystem including roles, requirements and processes used by the CAWG <<governing-authority,governing authority>> to achieve greater assurance over the provenance and authenticity of digital asset content.

[[certification-authority]]Certification authority (or CA)::
+
--
A trusted entity that issues, signs, and revokes digital certificates that bind <<public-key,public keys>> to subscriber identities.
<<certification-authority,CAs>> are also known as <<public-key-infrastructure,PKI>> <<certificate,certificate>> Authorities because they issue certificates based on public key infrastructure (<<public-key-infrastructure,PKI>>).
These certificates contain credentials that confirm the possession of a <<private-key,private key>> by an entity, among other verified attributes.
Individualas and organizations sign <<c2pa-manifest,C2PA manifests>> using digital signing credentials issued by <<certification-authority,CAs>>.

An entity on the <<cawg-trust-list,CAWG trust list>> that is trusted by the <<cawg-conformance-program,CAWG conformance program>> to issue X.509 <<claimsigning-certs,CAWG claim signing certificate>>s to instances of conforming <<_generator_product,generator products>>.

An organization that operates a <<certification-authority,certification authority>> may also operate a <<time-stamping-authority,time-stamping authority>>.
--
