[[amendments]]
=== Amendments

[[policy-modification]]
==== Policy modification

The CAWG <<governing-authority,governing authority>> retains the right to amend this <<certificate-policy,CP>> at any time, reflecting evolving security best practices, technological advancements, or regulatory changes.

[[amendments-subscriber-notification]]
==== Subscriber notification

<<subscriber,Subscribers>> MUST be promptly notified of any material changes to the <<certificate-policy,CP>> through appropriate channels, ensuring they remain informed of their obligations and responsibilities.

[[acceptance-of-amendments]]
==== Acceptance of amendments

Continued utilization of certificates after <<certificate-policy,CP>> amendments constitutes the <<subscriber,subscriber>>’s acceptance of the revised terms and conditions.
