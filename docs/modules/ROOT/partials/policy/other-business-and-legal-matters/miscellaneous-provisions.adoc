[[miscellaneous-provisions]]
=== Miscellaneous provisions

[[governing-agreements]]
==== Governing agreements

[[between-the-cawg-governing-authority-and-cas]]
===== Between the CAWG governing authority and CAs

The relationship between the CAWG <<governing-authority,governing authority>> and the <<certification-authority,CAs>> MUST be governed by the <<certification-authority,certification authority>> agreement and this <<certificate-policy,certificate policy>>.

[[between-cas-and-subscribers]]
===== Between CAs and subscribers

The relationship between the <<certification-authority,CA>> and the <<subscriber,subscriber>> MUST be governed by the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s <<subscriber-agreement,subscriber agreement>>.

[[waiver]]
==== Waiver

No stipulation.

[[severability]]
==== Severability

In the event that any provision of this <<certificate-policy,CP>> is deemed invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions MUST remain in full force and effect, and the invalid or unenforceable provision MUST be replaced with a valid and enforceable provision that most closely approximates the intent of the original provision.
