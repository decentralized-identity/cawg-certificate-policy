[[subscriber-representations]]
==== Subscriber representations

By applying for and utilizing certificates under this <<certificate-policy,CP>>, the <<subscriber,subscriber>> represents and warrants the following:

. The information furnished in the certificate application is accurate, complete, and truthful.
. They possess the requisite legal authority to request and utilize certificates under this <<certificate-policy,CP>>.
. They MUST adhere to all stipulations and restrictions delineated in this <<certificate-policy,CP>>.
. They MUST employ the certificates solely for their intended purpose, refraining from any prohibited activities.
