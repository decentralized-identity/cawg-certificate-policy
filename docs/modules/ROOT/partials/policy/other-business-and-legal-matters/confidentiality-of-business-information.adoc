[[confidentiality-of-business-information]]
=== Confidentiality of business information

[[cas-commitment-to-confidentiality]]
==== CA’s commitment to confidentiality

The <<certification-authority,CA>> pledges to uphold the confidentiality of all non-public <<subscriber,subscriber>> business information obtained during the certificate application and validation processes.

[[information-sharing-restrictions]]
==== Information sharing restrictions

Such information MUST NOT be shared with any third parties without the <<subscriber,subscriber>>’s explicit written consent, except when mandated by law or regulation, or required for independent auditing purposes.
