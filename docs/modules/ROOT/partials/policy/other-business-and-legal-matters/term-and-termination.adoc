[[term-and-termination]]
=== Term and termination

[[term]]
==== Term

This <<certificate-policy,CP>> becomes effective when approved by the CAWG <<governing-authority,governing authority>>.
Amendments to this <<certificate-policy,CP>> become effective upon publication.
This <<certificate-policy,CP>> has no specified term.

[[termination]]
==== Termination

This <<certificate-policy,CP>>, as amended from time to time, MUST remain in force until it is replaced by a new version.
Termination of this <<certificate-policy,CP>> is at the discretion of the CAWG <<governing-authority,governing authority>>.

[[termination-rights]]
==== Termination rights

No stipulation.

[[post-termination-obligations]]
==== Post-termination obligations

No stipulation.
