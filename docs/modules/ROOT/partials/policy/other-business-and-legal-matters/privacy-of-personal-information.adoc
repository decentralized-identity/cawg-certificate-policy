[[privacy-of-personal-information]]
=== Privacy of personal information

[[compliance-with-privacy-laws]]
==== Compliance with privacy laws

The <<certification-authority,CA>> commits to collecting and processing personal information in strict accordance with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

[[purpose-limitation]]
==== Purpose limitation

Personal information MUST be collected and utilized solely for the purposes explicitly outlined in this <<certificate-policy,CP>>, including but not limited to identity verification and certificate issuance.

[[data-subject-rights]]
==== Data subject rights

<<subscriber,Subscribers>> retain the right to access, rectify, or erase their personal information held by the <<certification-authority,CA>>, subject to legal and regulatory constraints.
