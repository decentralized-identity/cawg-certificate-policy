[[fees]]
=== Fees

[[fees-for-services]]
==== Fees for services

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY levy reasonable fees for services rendered under this <<certificate-policy,CP>>, encompassing certificate issuance, renewal, revocation, and technical support.
The fee structure MUST be transparently published and readily accessible to <<subscriber,subscribers>>.

[[fee-adjustments]]
==== Fee adjustments

The <<certification-authority,CA>> reserves the right to modify its fee structure with prior notification to <<subscriber,subscribers>>, ensuring transparency and predictability in cost management.

[[subscriber-responsibility]]
==== Subscriber responsibility

The <<subscriber,subscriber>> assumes full responsibility for any and all fees incurred under this <<certificate-policy,CP>>.
