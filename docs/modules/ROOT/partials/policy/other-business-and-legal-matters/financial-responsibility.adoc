[[financial-responsibility]]
=== Financial responsibility

[[subscribers-financial-obligation]]
==== Subscriber’s financial obligation

The <<subscriber,subscriber>> bears the financial responsibility for all fees associated with the certificate lifecycle, including issuance, renewal, and any potential revocation fees.

[[financial-responsibility-cas-limited-liability]]
==== CA’s limited liability

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST NOT be held liable for any financial losses or damages incurred by the <<subscriber,subscriber>> as a consequence of certificate misuse, compromise, or revocation, unless such losses directly arise from the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s negligence or willful misconduct.
