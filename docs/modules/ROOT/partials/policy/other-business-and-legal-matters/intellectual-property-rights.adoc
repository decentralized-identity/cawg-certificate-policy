[[intellectual-property-rights]]
=== Intellectual property rights

[[subscribers-ownership]]
==== Subscriber’s ownership

The <<subscriber,subscriber>> maintains full ownership and control over all intellectual property rights associated with the claim generation implementation that signs using certificates issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>>.

[[cas-non-claim]]
==== CA’s non-claim

The <<certification-authority,CA>> asserts no claim or ownership over any intellectual property rights pertaining to the <<subscriber,subscriber>>’s implementation.
