=== Repositories

[IMPORTANT,role=todo]
====
There are two subsections titled "Repositories" here. Can we merge them?
====

A <<certification-authority,CA>> issuing certificates under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> MUST establish and maintain an internal, secure repository containing records of all certificates issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>>.
A record for an issued certificate MUST be stored for at least 1 year after its expiry date.
Records in this repository MUST include essential details such as certificate serial numbers, subject names, validity periods, X.509v3 extensions and their values, and revocation status.
The repository MUST be subject to stringent access controls and regular audits to ensure its integrity and confidentiality.
