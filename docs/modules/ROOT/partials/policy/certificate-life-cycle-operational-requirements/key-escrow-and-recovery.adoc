[[key-escrow-and-recovery]]
=== Key escrow and recovery

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY escrow the <<subscriber,subscriber>>’s <<private-key,private key>> as specified in the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s <<certificate-policy,CP>> and/or <<certification-practice-statement,CPS>>.

[IMPORTANT,role=todo]
====
Critical, key escrow: escrow suits S/MIME encryption keys but defeats attribution for a signing key, because if the CA holds a copy the signature no longer proves the subscriber made it.
It also contradicts the MUST NOT be exported rule.

Suggest: Subscriber signing keys MUST NOT be escrowed, archived or backed up by the CA or any RA.
The same applies to the <<private-key,private key>> Escrow section and the validity section that lets the CA keep escrowed keys after expiry.
====

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST notify <<subscriber,subscribers>> when their <<private-key,private keys>> are escrowed.
Escrowed <<private-key,private keys>> MUST be stored in encrypted form.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST protect escrowed <<private-key,private keys>> from unauthorized disclosure.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST recover <<subscriber,subscriber>> <<private-key,private keys>> only under the circumstances permitted within the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s <<certificate-policy,CP>> and/or <<certification-practice-statement,CPS>>.
