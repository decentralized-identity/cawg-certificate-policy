[[certificate-acceptance]]
=== Certificate acceptance

. <<certificate,Certificate>> acceptance by the <<subscriber,subscriber>> MUST indicate the <<subscriber,subscriber>>’s acceptance of the terms of a <<subscriber-agreement,subscriber agreement>> that includes required stipulations of this <<certificate-policy,CP>>.
. The <<subscriber,subscriber>> MUST accept the <<certificate,certificate>> explicitly, by agreeing to the <<subscriber-agreement,subscriber agreement>> or <<terms-of-use,terms of use>>.
Installing or using the <<certificate,certificate>> in software does not by itself constitute acceptance.
. <<certificate,Certificates>> issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> are to be used exclusively by the individuals or organizations named as subjects of the certificates, to digitally sign <<cawg-identity-assertion,CAWG identity assertions>>.
Any usage for any other purpose is strictly prohibited.
