[[certificate-acceptance]]
=== Certificate acceptance

. <<certificate,Certificate>> acceptance by the <<subscriber,subscriber>> MUST indicate the <<subscriber,subscriber>>’s acceptance of the terms of a <<subscriber-agreement,subscriber agreement>> that includes required stipulations of this <<certificate-policy,CP>>.
. The acceptance MUST be implicit through successful installation and use of the certificate by an instance of the generator product.
+
[IMPORTANT,role=todo]
====
C2PA leftover: implicit acceptance by a generator product is the device model.
A person or organization should accept the certificate explicitly through the Subscriber Agreement.
====
. <<certificate,Certificates>> issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> are to be used exclusively by individual or organizations named as subjects of the certificates, to digitally sign CAWG claims at the <<assurance-level,assurance level>> indicated by the certificate.
Any usage for any other purpose is strictly prohibited.
