[[key-pair-and-certificate-usage]]
=== Key pair and certificate usage

. <<private-key,Private keys>> corresponding to root certificates MUST NOT be used to create signatures except for the following purposes:
.. Self-signed certificates to represent the <<root-certification-authority,root CA>> itself;
.. <<certificate,Certificates>> for <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CAs>> and cross-certified <<certification-authority,CA>> certificates;
.. <<certificate,Certificates>> for <<ocsp-responder,OCSP responders>>; and
.. <<certificate-revocation-list,Certificate revocation lists>>.
. <<private-key,Private keys>> corresponding to intermediate <<certification-authority,CA>> certificates where the Basic Constraints contains `pathLenContraint=1` MUST NOT be used to sign certificates except in the following cases:
.. <<certificate,Certificates>> for <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CAs>>
.. <<certificate,Certificates>> for infrastructure purposes (administrative role certificates, internal <<certification-authority,CA>> operational device certificates); and
.. <<certificate,Certificates>> for <<online-certificate-status-protocol,OCSP>> response verification.
. <<subscriber,Subscriber>> claim signing and time stamping certificates MUST only be issued by <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CAs>> where the Basic Constraints contains a `pathLenConstraint=0`.
. The use of a specific key is determined by the key usage extension in the X.509 certificate.
. The <<private-key,private key>> associated with the public key to which a claim signing <<certificate,certificate>> has been issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> MUST remain under the exclusive control of the <<subscriber,subscriber>> and MUST NOT be exported.
. Claim signing <<certificate,certificates>> issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> are to be used exclusively by individuals or organizations named as subjects of the certificates, to digitally sign CAWG claims at the <<assurance-level,assurance level>> indicated by the certificate.
Any usage for any other purpose is strictly prohibited.
. Time stamping certificates issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> are to be used exclusively by the time stamping authority or unit named as subjects of the certificates to provide time stamping services.
. <<ocsp-responder,OCSP responder>> certificates issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> are to be used exclusively to sign <<online-certificate-status-protocol,OCSP>> responses for certificates issued by the <<ocsp-responder,OCSP responder>> certificate’s <<issuing-ca,issuing CA>>.
. Any other use of a <<private-key,private key>> or certificate shall be deemed as misuse and its corresponding certificate subject to revocation per <<certificate-revocation-list,certificate revocation>>.
