[[records-archival]]
=== Records archival

[[types-of-records-archived]]
==== Types of records archived

The <<certification-authority,CA>> and each <<delegated-third-party,delegated third party>> MUST archive all audit logs (as set forth in <<types-of-events-recorded>>).

Additionally, the <<certification-authority,CA>> and each <<delegated-third-party,delegated third party>> MUST archive:

. documentation related to the security of their <<certificate,certificate>> systems, <<certificate,certificate>> management systems, <<root-certification-authority,root CA>> systems, and <<delegated-third-party,delegated third party>> systems; and
. documentation related to their verification, issuance, and revocation of <<certificate-request,certificate requests>> and <<certificate,certificates>>.

[[retention-period-for-archive]]
==== Retention period for archive

Archived audit logs (as set forth in <<types-of-records-archived>>, MUST be retained for a period of at least two (2) years from their record creation timestamp, or as long as they are required to be retained per <<retention-period-for-audit-log>>, whichever is longer.

Additionally, the <<certification-authority,CA>> and each <<delegated-third-party,delegated third party>> MUST retain, for at least two (2) years:

. all archived documentation related to the security of <<certificate,certificate>> systems, <<certificate,certificate>> management systems, <<root-certification-authority,root CA>> systems and <<delegated-third-party,delegated third party>> systems (as set forth in <<types-of-records-archived>>); and
. all archived documentation relating to the verification, issuance, and revocation of <<certificate-request,certificate requests>> and <<certificate,certificates>> (as set forth in <<types-of-records-archived>>) after the later occurrence of:
.. such records and documentation were last relied upon in the verification, issuance, or revocation of <<certificate-request,certificate requests>> and <<certificate,certificates>>; or
.. the expiration of the <<subscriber,subscriber>> <<certificate,certificates>> relying upon such records and documentation.

NOTE: While these requirements set the minimum retention period, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY choose a greater value as more appropriate in order to be able to investigate possible security or other types of incidents that will require retrospection and examination of past records archived.
