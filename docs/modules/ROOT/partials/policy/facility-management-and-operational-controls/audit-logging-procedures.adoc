[[audit-logging-procedures]]
=== Audit logging procedures

[[types-of-events-recorded]]
==== Types of events recorded

The <<certification-authority,CA>> and each <<delegated-third-party,delegated third party>> MUST record events related to the security of their <<certificate,certificate>> systems, <<certificate,certificate>> management systems, <<root-certification-authority,root CA>> systems, and <<delegated-third-party,delegated third party>> systems.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> and each <<delegated-third-party,delegated third party>> MUST record events related to their actions taken to process a <<certificate-request,certificate request>> and to issue a <<certificate,certificate>>, including all information generated and documentation received in connection with the <<certificate-request,certificate request>>; the time and date; and the personnel involved.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST make these records available to its <<qualified-auditor,qualified auditor>> as proof of the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s compliance with these requirements.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST record at least the following events:

. <<certification-authority,CA>> <<certificate,certificate>> and key lifecycle events, including:

.. key generation, backup, storage, recovery, archival, and destruction;
.. <<certificate-request,certificate requests>>, renewal, and re-key requests, and revocation;
.. approval and rejection of <<certificate-request,certificate requests>>;
.. cryptographic device lifecycle management events;
.. generation of <<certificate-revocation-list,certificate revocation lists>>;
.. signing of <<online-certificate-status-protocol,OCSP>> responses (as described in <<certificate-revocation-and-suspension>> and [.line-through]##certificate status services##; and

[[router-and-firewall-activities-logs]]
===== Router and firewall activities logs

Logging of router and firewall activities necessary to meet the requirements of <<types-of-events-recorded>> MUST at a minimum include:

. successful and unsuccessful login attempts to routers and firewalls; and
. logging of all administrative actions performed on routers and firewalls, including configuration changes, firmware updates, and access control modifications; and
. logging of all changes made to firewall rules, including additions, modifications, and deletions; and
. logging of all system events and errors, including hardware failures, software crashes, and system restarts.

[[vulnerability-assessments]]
==== Vulnerability assessments

The <<certification-authority,CA>>’s security program MUST include an annual risk assessment that:

. identifies foreseeable internal and external threats that could result in unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, alteration, or destruction of any <<certificate-data,certificate data>> or <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>;
. assesses the likelihood and potential damage of these threats, taking into consideration the sensitivity of the <<certificate-data,certificate data>> and <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>; and
. assesses the sufficiency of the policies, procedures, information systems, technology, and other arrangements that the <<certification-authority,CA>> has in place to counter such threats.
