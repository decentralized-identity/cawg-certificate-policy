[[personnel-controls]]
=== Personnel controls

[[clearances]]
==== Clearances

The <<certification-authority,CA>>’s policies and procedures MUST specify the background checks and clearance procedures required for <<trusted-role,trusted roles>> and non-trusted roles.
As a minimum, verification checks on permanent staff MUST be performed at the time of job application and every five years for those individuals undertaking <<trusted-role,trusted roles>>.

[[training-and-awareness]]
==== Training and awareness

. All personnel involved in <<certification-authority,CA>> operations MUST undergo regular security awareness training, including <<public-key-infrastructure,PKI>> concepts, data protection, incident response, attestation validation (if applicable), and their specific roles and responsibilities.
. Periodic retraining MUST occur to verify the continued trustworthiness of personnel involved in the activities related to key management and certificate management.
This training MUST be performed if there is a material change in <<public-key-infrastructure,PKI>> operation.

[[access-control]]
==== Access control

. Access to <<certification-authority,CA>> systems and data MUST be granted on a need-to-know basis, adhering to the principle of least privilege.
. Strong password policies, multi-factor authentication, and regular access reviews MUST be implemented.
