[[key-changeover]]
=== Key changeover

[[key-changeover-process]]
==== Key changeover process

. A secure and documented process MUST be in place for the periodic rotation of <<certification-authority,CA>> keys.
. The changeover process should minimize disruption to <<subscriber,subscribers>> and maintain the chain of trust.
. <<certification-authority,CAs>> SHOULD encourage <<subscriber,subscribers>> to retain key rotation agility.

[[key-changeover-subscriber-notification]]
==== Subscriber notification

<<subscriber,Subscribers>> SHOULD be notified in advance of any planned key changeover events, allowing them sufficient time to update their systems and configurations.
