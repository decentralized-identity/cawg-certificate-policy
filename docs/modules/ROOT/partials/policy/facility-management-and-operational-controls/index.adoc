== Facility, management, and operational controls

This section details the physical, procedural, and personnel controls necessary to safeguard the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s operations and maintain the integrity of the <<public-key-infrastructure,PKI>> system, particularly as it relates to the issuance and management of certificates.
While physical security remains important, the emphasis here is on logical and procedural controls, reflecting the nature of the environment where these certificates will be used.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST develop, implement, and maintain a comprehensive security program designed to:

. protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of <<certificate-data,certificate data>> and <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>;
. protect against anticipated threats or hazards to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the <<certificate-data,certificate data>> and <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>;
. protect against unauthorized or unlawful access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of any <<certificate-data,certificate data>> or <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>;
. protect against accidental loss or destruction of, or damage to, any <<certificate-data,certificate data>> or <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>; and
. comply with all other security requirements applicable to the <<certification-authority,CA>> by law.

The <<certificate-management-process,certificate management process>> MUST include:

. physical security and environmental controls;
. system integrity controls, including configuration management, integrity maintenance of trusted code, and malware detection/prevention;
. network security and firewall management, including port restrictions and IP address filtering;
. user management, separate trusted-role assignments, education, awareness, and training; and
. logical access controls, activity logging, and inactivity time-outs to provide individual accountability.

The <<certification-authority,CA>>’s security program MUST include an annual risk assessment that:

. identifies foreseeable internal and external threats that could result in unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, alteration, or destruction of any <<certificate-data,certificate data>> or <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>;
. assesses the likelihood and potential damage of these threats, taking into consideration the sensitivity of the <<certificate-data,certificate data>> and <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>; and
. assesses the sufficiency of the policies, procedures, information systems, technology, and other arrangements that the <<certification-authority,CA>> has in place to counter such threats.

Based on the risk assessment, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST develop, implement, and maintain a security plan consisting of security procedures, measures, and products designed to achieve the objectives set forth above and to manage and control the risks identified during the risk assessment, commensurate with the sensitivity of the <<certificate-data,certificate data>> and <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>.
The security plan MUST include administrative, organizational, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the <<certificate-data,certificate data>> and <<certificate-management-process,certificate management processes>>.
The security plan MUST also take into account then-available technology and the cost of implementing the specific measures, and MUST implement a reasonable level of security appropriate to the harm that might result from a breach of security and the nature of the data to be protected.
