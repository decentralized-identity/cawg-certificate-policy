== Overview

This <<certificate-policy,certificate policy>> (<<certificate-policy,CP>>) establishes the requirements governing the issuance of <<cawg-identity-certificate,CAWG identity certificates>> to individuals and organizations abiding by the technical specifications developed by the https://cawg.io[Creator Assertions Working Group].

The policy sets the requirements that MUST be met by a <<certification-authority,certification authority>> (<<certification-authority,CA>>) in the process of issuing <<cawg-identity-certificate,CAWG identity certificates>> to <<subscriber,subscribers>> that intend to sign <<cawg-identity-assertion,CAWG identity assertions>>.
A <<cawg-identity-certificate,CAWG identity certificate>> identifies a verified person or organization.
Certificates under this policy are not issued to devices, software products, or software instances.

<<cawg-identity-certificate,CAWG identity certificates>> under this policy MUST only be issued by <<certification-authority,certification authorities>> that have successfully passed the <<cawg-conformance-program,CAWG conformance program>>, as documented through their inclusion in the <<cawg-trust-list,CAWG trust list>>.
Digital certificates issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> indicate the <<certificate-type,certificate type>> and the <<level-of-assurance,level of assurance (LOA)>>, which a <<relying-party,relying party>> can use as part of its trust evaluation of the <<subscriber,subscriber>> named in a <<cawg-identity-assertion,CAWG identity assertion>>.

<<certification-authority,Certification authorities>> that comply with this <<certificate-policy,certificate policy>> and want to issue certificates under the <<cawg-conformance-program,CAWG conformance program>> MUST apply to the CAWG <<governing-authority,governing authority>> for inclusion on the <<cawg-trust-list,CAWG trust list>>.

This <<certificate-policy,certificate policy>> considers the Baseline Requirements for the Issuance and Management of Publicly-Trusted S/MIME Certificates, as adopted by the CA/Browser Forum.

These requirements describe four <<certificate,certificate>> profiles differentiated by the type of <<subject,subject>> that apply to <<cawg-identity-certificate,CAWG identity certificates>>, grouped into two <<level-of-assurance,levels of assurance>>:

[options="header",cols="2,1,5"]
|===
| <<certificate-type,Certificate type>>
| <<level-of-assurance,LOA>>
| Description

| <<mailbox-validated,mailbox-validated>>
| LOA1
| Includes only a <<mailbox-address,mailbox address>>.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> has verified that the <<applicant,applicant>> controls the mailbox, and nothing else about their identity.

| organization-validated
| LOA2
| Includes only organizational (<<legal-entity,legal entity>>) attributes in the <<subject,subject>>.

| <<sponsor-validated,sponsor-validated>>
| LOA2
| Combines <<individual,individual>> (<<natural-person,natural person>>) attributes in conjunction with a `subject:organizationName` (an associated <<legal-entity,legal entity>>) attribute.
Registration for <<sponsor-validated,sponsor-validated>> <<certificate,certificates>> MAY be performed by an <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>>.

| <<individual,individual>>-validated
| LOA2
| Includes only <<individual,individual>> (<<natural-person,natural person>>) attributes in the <<subject,subject>>.
|===

At LOA1, a <<relying-party,relying party>> learns only that the <<signer,signer>> controlled the <<mailbox-address,mailbox address>> when the <<certificate,certificate>> was issued.
At LOA2, the <<certification-authority,CA>> has verified the identity of the organization and/or <<individual,individual>> named in the <<certificate,certificate>> as described in <<initial-identity-validation>>.
A <<relying-party,relying party>> SHOULD NOT present an LOA1 <<certificate,certificate>> as evidence of a verified personal or organizational identity.
