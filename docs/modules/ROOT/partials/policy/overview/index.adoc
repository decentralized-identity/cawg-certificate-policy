== Overview

This <<certificate-policy,certificate policy>> (<<certificate-policy,CP>>) establishes the requirements governing the issuance of <<claimsigning-certs,CAWG claim signing certificates>> for use by individuals or organizations abiding by the technical specifications developed by the https://cawg.io[Creator Assertions Working Group].

The policy sets the requirements that MUST be met by a <<certification-authority,certification authority>> (<<certification-authority,CA>>) in the process of issuing <<claimsigning-certs,CAWG claim signing certificates>> to <<subscriber,subscriber>>s that intends to make CAWG claims on digital assets.

<<claimsigning-certs,CAWG claim signing certificates>> under this policy MUST only be issued by <<certification-authority,certification authorities>> that have successfully passed the <<cawg-conformance-program,CAWG conformance program>>, as documented through their inclusion in the <<cawg-trust-list,CAWG trust list>>.
Digital certificates issued under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> indicate an <<assurance-level,assurance level>> that can be used by a <<relying-party,relying party>> as part of its trust evaluation of an <<subscriber,subscriber>> that makes claims on CAWG assertions.

<<certification-authority,Certification authorities>> that comply with this <<certificate-policy,certificate policy>> and want to issue certificates under the <<cawg-conformance-program,CAWG conformance program>> MUST apply to the CAWG <<governing-authority,governing authority>> for inclusion on the <<cawg-trust-list,CAWG trust list>>.

This <<certificate-policy,certificate policy>> considers the Baseline Requirements for the Issuance and Management of Publicly-Trusted S/MIME Certificates, as adopted by the CA/Browser Forum.

These requirements describe three <<certificate,certificate>> profiles differentiated by the type of <<subject,subject>> that apply to CAWG claim-signing certificates:

[options="header",cols="2,5"]
|===
| <<certificate-type,Certificate type>>
| Description

| organization-validated
| Includes only organizational (<<legal-entity,legal entity>>) attributes in the <<subject,subject>>.

| <<sponsor-validated,sponsor-validated>>
| Combines <<individual,individual>> (<<natural-person,natural person>>) attributes in conjunction with a `subject:organizationName` (an associated <<legal-entity,legal entity>>) attribute.
Registration for <<sponsor-validated,sponsor-validated>> <<certificate,certificates>> MAY be performed by an <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>>.

| <<individual,individual>>-validated
| Includes only <<individual,individual>> (<<natural-person,natural person>>) attributes in the <<subject,subject>>.
|===
