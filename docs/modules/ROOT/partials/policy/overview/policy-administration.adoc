=== Policy administration

This <<certificate-policy,CP>> is administered by the <<administering-authority,administering authority>> on behalf of the <<governing-authority,governing authority>>.

[IMPORTANT,role=todo]
====
This makes the Trust Task Force the Administering Authority that operates the program.
That conflicts with the 2 Oct Friends of CAWG decision that CAWG designates and sets requirements but does not operate registries or conformance programs.
Suggest a Program Administrator independent of applicants, appointed by the Steering Committee as Governing Authority, with two-person approval for list changes.
====

[[cp-approval-procedures]]
==== CP approval procedures

The <<governing-authority,governing authority>> approves the <<certificate-policy,CP>> and any amendments.
Amendments are made either by updating the entire <<certificate-policy,CP>> or by publishing an addendum.
The <<governing-authority,governing authority>> determines whether an amendment to this <<certificate-policy,CP>> requires notice or an <<object-identifier,OID>> change.
