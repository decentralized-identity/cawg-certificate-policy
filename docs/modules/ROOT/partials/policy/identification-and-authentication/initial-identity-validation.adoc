[[initial-identity-validation]]
=== Initial identity validation

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST authenticate the identity attributes of the <<subject,subject>> to be included in the S/MIME <<certificate,certificate>> according to the requirements of the following sections:

[options="header",cols="4,3,3"]
|===
| <<certificate-type,Certificate type>>
| Organization identity
| <<individual,Individual>> identity

| `Organization-validated`
| xref:authentication-of-organization-identity[xrefstyle=short]
| N/A

| `Sponsor-validated`
| xref:authentication-of-organization-identity[xrefstyle=short]
| xref:authentication-of-individual-identity[xrefstyle=short]

| `Individual-validated`
| N/A
| xref:authentication-of-individual-identity[xrefstyle=short]
|===

[[authentication-of-organization-identity]]
==== Authentication of organization identity

The following requirements MUST be fulfilled to authenticate organization identity included in the `Organization-validated` and `Sponsor-validated` profiles.

[[attribute-collection-of-organization-identity]]
===== Attribute collection of organization identity

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST collect and retain evidence supporting the following identity attributes for the organization:

. Formal name of the <<legal-entity,legal entity>>;
. A registered <<assumed-name,assumed name>> for the <<legal-entity,legal entity>> (if included in the <<subject,subject>>);
. An <<affiliate,affiliate>> of the <<legal-entity,legal entity>> as described in <<subscriber-subject-distinguished-name-fields>> (if included in the <<subject,subject>> as an `subject:organizationalUnitName`);
. An address of the <<legal-entity,legal entity>> (if included in the <<subject,subject>>);
. <<jurisdiction-of-incorporation,Jurisdiction of incorporation>> or registration of the <<legal-entity,legal entity>>; and
. Identifier and type of identifier for the <<legal-entity,legal entity>>.

The identifier MUST be included in the <<certificate,certificate>> `subject:organizationIdentifier` as specified in <<subscriber-subject-distinguished-name-fields>> and <<appendix-a---registration-schemes, Appendix A>>.

[[validation-of-organization-identity]]
===== Validation of organization identity

If an <<attestation,attestation>> is used as evidence for the validation of the attributes described in this section, then the <<attestation,attestation>> MUST be verified for authenticity as described in <<reliability-of-verification-sources>>.

[[verification-of-name-address-and-identifier]]
====== Verification of name, address, and identifier

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST verify the full legal name and an address (if included in the <<certificate,certificate>> <<subject,subject>>) of the <<legal-entity,legal entity>> <<applicant,applicant>> using documentation provided by, or through communication with, at least one of the following:

. A government agency in the jurisdiction of the <<legal-entity,legal entity>>’s creation, existence, or recognition;
. A <<legal-entity-identifier,legal entity identifier (LEI)>> data reference;
. A site visit by the <<certification-authority,CA>> or a third party who is acting as an agent for the <<certification-authority,CA>>; or
. An <<attestation,attestation>> which includes a copy of supporting documentation used to establish the <<applicant,applicant>>’s legal existence (such as a certificate of registration, articles of incorporation, operating agreement, statute, or regulatory act) and its current status.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY use the same documentation or communication described in 1 through 4 above to verify both the <<applicant,applicant>>’s identity and address.

In cases 1 and 4 above, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST verify that the status of the <<applicant,applicant>> is not designated by labels such as “ceased,” “inactive,” “invalid,” “not current,” or the equivalent.

In case 2 above when <<legal-entity-identifier,LEI>> data reference is used, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST verify that the `RegistrationStatus` is `ISSUED` and the `EntityStatus` is `ACTIVE`.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST only allow use of an <<legal-entity-identifier,LEI>> if the `ValidationSources` entry is `FULLY_CORROBORATED`.
An <<legal-entity-identifier,LEI>> MUST NOT be used if the `ValidationSources` entry is `PARTIALLY_CORROBORATED`, `PENDING`, or `ENTITY_SUPPLIED_ONLY`.

[[verification-of-assumed-name]]
====== Verification of assumed name

<<applicant,Applicants>> MAY request an <<assumed-name,assumed name>> to be included in the <<certificate,certificate>>.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST verify that:

. The <<applicant,applicant>> has registered its use of the <<assumed-name,assumed name>> with the appropriate government agency for such filings in the jurisdiction of its incorporation or registration; and
. The <<assumed-name,assumed name>> filing continues to be valid.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY rely on an <<attestation,attestation>> that indicates the <<assumed-name,assumed name>> under which the <<applicant,applicant>> conducts business, the government agency with which the <<assumed-name,assumed name>> is registered, and that such filing continues to be valid.

[[disclosure-of-verification-sources]]
===== Disclosure of verification sources

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST verify the <<registration-reference,registration reference>> to be included in the <<certificate,certificate>> from a register that is maintained or authorized by the relevant government agency.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST disclose the authorized sources it uses to verify the <<applicant,applicant>>’s creation, existence, or recognition.
This disclosure MUST be through an appropriate and readily accessible online means.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST document where to obtain this information within Section 3.2 of the <<certification-authority,CA>>’s <<certificate-policy,CP>> and/<<certification-practice-statement,or CPS>>.

Nothing in these requirements prohibits the use of third-party vendors to obtain regularly-updated and current information from the government register provided that the third party obtains the information directly from the government.

In the case of a <<legal-entity-identifier,LEI>> data reference, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST verify the associated data record with the https://search.gleif.org/#/search/[Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation].

[[authentication-of-individual-identity]]
==== Authentication of individual identity

The following requirements MUST be fulfilled to authenticate <<individual,individual>> identity attributes included in `Sponsor-validated` and `Individual-validated` <<certificate,certificate>> profiles.

The <<certification-authority,CA>>, <<registration-authority,RA>>, or <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>> MUST collect and retain evidence supporting the following identity attributes for the <<individual,individual>> <<applicant,applicant>>:

. Given name(s) and surname(s), which MUST be current names;
. <<pseudonym,Pseudonym>> (if used);
. Title (if used);
. Address (if displayed in <<subject,subject>>); and
. Further information as needed to uniquely identify the <<applicant,applicant>>.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST comply with applicable data protection legislation in the gathering and retention of evidence relating to <<individual,individual>> identity supporting this requirement in accordance with <<information-treated-as-private>>.

[[attribute-collection-of-individual-identity]]
===== Attribute collection of individual identity

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST document and publish the methods it uses to collect <<individual,individual>> identity attributes.

[[from-a-physical-identity-document]]
====== From a physical identity document

If physical identity documents are used to provide evidence, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST accept only government-issued passports or identity cards, and other official identity documents of comparable reliability (such as drivers license or military ID).

The physical identity document used as evidence MUST contain a face photo and/or other information that can be compared with the <<applicant,applicant>>’s physical appearance.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST document and publish information describing the physical or digital identity documents or document types it accepts.

[[from-a-digital-identity-document]]
====== From a digital identity document

If digital identity documents (such as government-issued passports or national ID cards including a chip bearing digitally signed information about the holder) are used to provide evidence, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST only accept eMRTD digital identity documents in compliance with ICAO 9303 part 10.

This method does not include “<<electronic-identification,eID>>” as described in Regulation (EU) 910/2014.

[[using-electronic-identification-schemes-eid]]
====== Using electronic identification schemes (eID)

If an <<electronic-identification,eID>> is used to provide evidence, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST only accept “notified” <<electronic-identification,eID>> schemes according to Article 9 of the https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/2024-05-20[eIDAS Regulation] and the <<electronic-identification,eID>> shall conform to eIDAS LoA “Substantial” or “High.”

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST document and publish information describing the <<electronic-identification,eID>> and associated <<electronic-identification,eID>> attributes it accepts.

[[from-a-certificate-supporting-a-digital-signature-applied-by-the-applicant]]
====== From a certificate supporting a digital signature applied by the applicant

If a digital signature is used to provide evidence, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST have the <<applicant,applicant>> digitally sign the <<certificate-request,certificate request>> using a valid personal <<certificate,certificate>> that was issued under an approved framework described in <<approved-frameworks>>.

Identity attributes are evidenced by the signing <<certificate,certificate>>, not by the content of the signed document.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST only rely upon the signing <<certificate,certificate>> as evidence for identity attributes if the digital signature is valid in accordance with the requirements of the relevant <<approved-frameworks,approved framework>>.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> SHOULD consider requirements to avoid issuance of consecutive <<certificate,certificates>> that are issued based on a preceding <<certificate,certificate>>, where the original verification of the <<subject,subject>>’s identity may have been conducted in the distant past.

[[from-enterprise-ra-records]]
====== From enterprise RA records

In the case of `Sponsor-validated` <<certificate,certificates>> approved by an <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>>, records maintained by the <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>> MUST be accepted as evidence of <<individual,individual>> identity.

The <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>> MUST maintain records to satisfy the requirements of <<registration-authority>> and <<review-of-delegated-parties>>.

[[affiliation-from-company-attestation]]
====== Affiliation from company attestation

In the case of `Sponsor-validated` <<certificate,certificates>> not approved by an <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>>, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY verify the authority or affiliation of an <<individual,individual>> to represent an organization to be included in the `subject:organizationName` of the <<certificate,certificate>> using an <<attestation,attestation>> provided by the organization and verified in accordance with <<reliability-of-verification-sources>>.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST still verify the identity of the <<individual,individual>> in accordance with <<authentication-of-individual-identity>> and the organization in accordance with <<authentication-of-organization-identity>>.

[[from-a-general-attestation]]
====== From a general attestation

Evidence for <<individual,individual>> identity attributes MAY be gathered using an <<attestation,attestation>> from a qualified legal practitioner or notary in the <<applicant,applicant>>’s jurisdiction.

[[from-authorized-reference-sources-as-supplementary-evidence]]
====== From authorized reference sources as supplementary evidence

Evidence for <<individual,individual>> identity attributes MUST use at least one of the following sources for authoritative evidence:  a physical or digital identity document, an eligible <<electronic-identification,eID>>, an eligible <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>>, digital signature supported by certificate, <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>> records, or suitable <<attestation,attestation>>.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY additionally gather and verify supplementary evidence using authorized sources such as additional official documents, government or regulatory registers, or national population registers.

Examples of this method include:

* If the <<subject,subject>> presents an ID featuring an <<applicant,applicant>> name that has subsequently been changed, the evidence MAY be complemented by inspection of an official document such as a marriage certificate or court order documenting the change.
* If a professional title of a regulated profession in the `subject:country`, or a corporate title linked to the `subject:organizationName`, is to be used it MUST be verified against supporting documentation, a <<reliable-data-source,reliable data source>>, or <<attestation,attestation>>.
* In cases where the “role” <<legal-entity-identifier,LEI>> is included in an extension of a `Sponsor-validated` <<certificate,certificate>>, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST verify that the <<legal-entity-identifier,LEI>> is assigned to the <<individual,individual>> and the `subject:organizationName` in the <<certificate,certificate>> <<subject,subject>>.
* The <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY verify the address (but not the identity) of the <<applicant,applicant>> using a utility bill, bank statement, credit card statement, government-issued tax document, or other form of identification that the <<certification-authority,CA>> determines to be reliable.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST internally document the accepted reference sources, including a description of the documents or <<attestation,attestations>> accepted as supplementary evidence.

[[from-a-mobile-driver-license-mdl]]
====== From a mobile driver license (mDL)

If an <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> is used to provide evidence, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST only accept an <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> that:

* is issued by an <<issuing-authority,issuing authority>> that is legally authorized by the relevant government or jurisdiction to issue driving licenses;
* conforms to ISO/IEC 18013-5;
* supports validation through the verification protocols defined in ISO/IEC 18013-7; and
* can demonstrate a valid <<certificate,certificate>> chain from the Document <<signer,signer>> <<certificate,certificate>> to an <<issuing-authority,issuing authority>> <<certificate,certificate>> that is verifiable through a publicly available trust anchor or trust list approved by the issuing jurisdiction.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST document and publish information describing the issung jurisdictions and <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> versions it accepts.

[[validation-of-individual-identity]]
===== Validation of individual identity

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST validate all identity attributes of the <<individual,individual>> to be included in the <<certificate,certificate>>.

If the evidence has an explicit validity period, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST verify that the time of the identity validation is within this validity period.
In context this can include the `notBefore` and `notAfter` fields of a digital signature <<certificate,certificate>> or the date of expiry of an identity document.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY reuse existing evidence to validate <<individual,individual>> identity subject to the age restrictions in <<performing-identification-and-authentication-functions>>.

[[validation-of-a-physical-identity-document]]
====== Validation of a physical identity document

The physical identity document MUST be presented in its original form.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST employ procedures to ensure the evidence presented by the <<applicant,applicant>> is a genuine identity document that is not counterfeited or falsified/modified.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY use manual (in person) or remote procedures.
A remote process MUST ensure that the <<applicant,applicant>> has the document in hand and presents the document in real-time in front of a camera.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> registration agent MUST make a visual comparison of the physical appearance of the <<applicant,applicant>> and the face photo and/or other information on the physical identity document.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> registration agent MUST have access to authoritative sources of information on document appearance and validation for forms of identity document accepted by the <<certification-authority,CA>>.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST retain information sufficient to evidence the fulfillment of the identity validation process and the verified attributes.
In addition to identity attributes, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST record the following information: issuer, validity period, and the document’s unique identification number.

Automated and manual processes MAY be used in combination, (for example the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> may deploy automated tools to support the work of a registration agent, or an automated process that falls back to a registration agent if the process yields an uncertain result).

[[validation-of-a-digital-identity-document]]
====== Validation of a digital identity document

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST only accept digital identity documents if the issuer’s digital signature on the document is successfully validated against the relevant country signing <<certificate,certificates>> (e.g., from the https://www.icao.int/icao-pkd[ICAO public key database]).

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST record information obtained from the digital identity document to evidence the identity proofing process.
In addition to identity attributes and face photo, the following information MUST be recorded: issuer, validity period, and the document’s unique identification number.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> registration agent MUST make a visual comparison of the physical appearance of the <<applicant,applicant>> and the face photo and/or other information on the digital identity document.

Automated and manual processes MAY be used in combination. (For example, using automated tools to support the work of a registration agent, or an automated process that falls back to a registration agent if the process yields an uncertain result.)

[[validation-of-eid]]
====== Validation of eID

If authentication using an <<electronic-identification,eID>> is used to provide evidence, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST confirm that the <<electronic-identification,eID>> scheme is suitable (i.e., that the <<electronic-identification,eID>> is accessible via a “notified” eIDAS-Node), and that the individual <<electronic-identification,eID>> is valid (i.e., not expired, suspended, or revoked).

The authentication using the <<electronic-identification,eID>> MUST be created as part of the identity validation process, and evidence of the validation with the <<electronic-identification,eID>>’s Identity Provider (IdP) MUST be retained by the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>>.

[[validation-of-digital-signature-with-certificate]]
====== Validation of digital signature with certificate

If a digital signature with <<certificate,certificate>> is used to provide evidence, the signature MUST be created as part of the identity validation process.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST validate the digital signature and MUST only use the signing <<certificate,certificate>> as evidence for identity attributes if the signature is valid.

If required identity attributes to be collected are not present in the <<certificate,certificate>>, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST collect these attributes from other sources and validate them accordingly.

[[validation-using-an-enterprise-ra-record]]
====== Validation using an enterprise RA record

An <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>> issuing a <<sponsor-validated,sponsor-validated>> <<certificate,certificate>> MUST validate all identity attributes of an <<individual,individual>> to be included in the <<certificate,certificate>>.
The <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>> MAY rely upon existing internal records to validate <<individual,individual>> identity.

[[validation-of-affiliation-from-a-company-attestation]]
====== Validation of affiliation from a company attestation

The reliability of the <<attestation,attestation>> MUST be verified according to <<reliability-of-verification-sources>>.

[[validation-of-a-general-attestation]]
====== Validation of a general attestation

The reliability of the <<attestation,attestation>> MUST be verified according to <<reliability-of-verification-sources>>.

[[validation-of-a-supplementary-evidence]]
====== Validation of a supplementary evidence

No stipulation.

[[validation-of-an-mdl]]
====== Validation of an mDL

If an <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> is used to provide evidence, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST:

* Authenticate to the <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> holder’s device and request data in conformance with ISO/IEC 18013-5 and ISO/IEC 18013-7;
* Verify the authenticity and integrity of the <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> data through validation of the Mobile Security Object (MSO) and its signature chain;
* Validate the document <<signer,signer>> <<certificate,certificate>> chain against the issuing jurisdiction’s published trust infrastructure; and
* Verify that the <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> is current and has not been revoked or expired.

The <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> authentication MUST be performed in real-time as part of the identity validation process, and MUST NOT rely on previously captured <<mobile-driver-license,mDL>> data.

[[non-verified-subscriber-information]]
==== Non-verified subscriber information

<<subscriber,Subscriber>> information that has not been verified in accordance with these requirements MUST NOT be included in publicly-trusted S/MIME <<certificate,certificates>>.

[[validation-of-authority]]
==== Validation of authority

Before commencing to issue `Organization-validated` and `Sponsor-validated` <<certificate,certificates>> for an <<applicant,applicant>>, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST use a <<reliable-method-of-communication,reliable method of communication>> to verify the authority and approval of an <<applicant,applicant>> representative to perform one or more of the following:

* to act as an <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>>;
* to request issuance or revocation of <<certificate,certificates>>; or
* to assign responsibilities to others to act in these roles.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY establish a process that allows an <<applicant,applicant>> to specify the individuals who may act as <<applicant,applicant>> representatives on an ongoing basis.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST provide an <<applicant,applicant>> with a list of its authorized <<applicant,applicant>> representatives upon the <<applicant,applicant>>’s verified written request.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY use the sources listed in <<attribute-collection-of-organization-identity>> to verify the <<reliable-method-of-communication,reliable method of communication>>.
Provided that the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> uses a <<reliable-method-of-communication,reliable method of communication>>, the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY establish the authenticity of the <<certificate-request,certificate request>> directly with the <<applicant,applicant>> representative or with an authoritative source within the <<applicant,applicant>>’s organization, such as the <<applicant,applicant>>’s main business offices, corporate offices, human resource offices, information technology offices, or other department that the <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> deems appropriate.

[[criteria-for-interoperation]]
==== Criteria for interoperation

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST disclose all <<cross-certificate,cross certificates>> that identify the <<certification-authority,CA>> as the <<subject,subject>>, provided that the <<certification-authority,CA>> arranged for or accepted the establishment of the trust relationship (i.e., the <<cross-certificate,cross certificate>> at issue).

[[reliability-of-verification-sources]]
==== Reliability of verification sources

Before relying on a source of verification data to validate <<certificate-request,certificate requests>>, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST verify its suitability as a <<reliable-data-source,reliable data source>>.
<<enterprise-ra,Enterprise RA>> records are a <<reliable-data-source,reliable data source>> for <<individual,individual>> <<subject,subject>> attributes included in <<sponsor-validated,sponsor-validated>> <<certificate,certificates>> issued to the <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>>’s organization.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MAY rely upon a letter attesting that <<subject-information,subject information>> or other fact is correct.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST verify that the letter was written by an accountant, lawyer, government official, or other reliable third party in the <<applicant,applicant>>’s jurisdiction customarily relied upon for such information.

An <<attestation,attestation>> MUST include a copy of documentation supporting the fact to be attested.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> or <<registration-authority,RA>> MUST use a <<reliable-method-of-communication,reliable method of communication>> to contact the sender and to confirm the <<attestation,attestation>> is authentic.
