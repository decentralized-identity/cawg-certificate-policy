[[identification-and-authentication-for-revocation-requests]]
=== Identification and authentication for revocation requests

Revocation requests MAY be initiated by the <<subscriber,subscriber>>, the <<certification-authority,CA>>, or the CAWG.
If the request is <<subscriber,subscriber>> or CAWG initiated, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST authenticate the requestor and verify the authenticity and validity of the revocation request.
Once authentication is completed and the authenticity and validity of the request are verified, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST revoke the <<certificate,certificate>> within the time limits set out in <<reasons-for-revoking-a-subscriber-certificate>>.
