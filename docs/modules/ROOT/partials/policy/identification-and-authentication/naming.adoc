[[types-of-names]]
==== Types of names

When the `subject:commonName` of a <<certificate,certificate>> issued to an <<individual,individual>> does not contain a mailbox address, it is specified as a <<personal-name,personal name>> or <<pseudonym,pseudonym>> as described in <<subscriber-subject-distinguished-name-fields>>(a).

Names consisting of multiple words are permitted.
Given names joined with a hyphen are considered as one single given name.
<<subject,Subjects>> with more than one given name MAY choose one or several of their given names in any sequence.
<<subject,Subjects>> MAY choose the order of their given name(s) and surname in accordance with national preference.

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY allow common variations or abbreviations of <<personal-name,personal names>> consistent with local practice.

[[the-use-of-meaningful-names]]
==== The use of meaningful names

<<personal-name,Personal names>> MUST be a meaningful representation of the <<subject,subject>>’s name as verified in the identifying documentation or <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>> records.

[[anonymity-or-pseudonymity-of-subscribers]]
==== Anonymity or pseudonymity of subscribers

The purpose of a <<pseudonym,pseudonym>> is to provide a unique identifier linked to an <<individual,individual>> in a pseudonymized manner when certain privacy conditions are required.
For example, a <<pseudonym,pseudonym>> may be used if a government agency requires officials to sign certain decisions via S/MIME so those decisions trace back to individuals, but emphasize the importance of the role over <<individual,individual>> identity in the <<certificate,certificate>>.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST disclose in its <<certificate-policy,CP>> and/<<certification-practice-statement,or CPS>> if it allows the use of <<pseudonym,pseudonyms>>.

For `Sponsor-validated` certificates, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY use a `subject:pseudonym` attribute in the <<certificate,certificate>> if the associated <<subject,subject>> has been verified according to <<authentication-of-individual-identity>>.
If present, the `subject:pseudonym` attribute MUST be:

. either a unique identifier selected by the <<certification-authority,CA>> for the <<subject,subject>> of the <<certificate,certificate>>; or
. an identifier selected by the <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RA>> which uniquely identifies the <<subject,subject>> of the <<certificate,certificate>> within the organization included in the `subject:organizationName` attribute.

For `Individual-validated` certificates, the <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY use the `subject:pseudonym` attribute if the associated <<subject,subject>> has been verified according to <<authentication-of-individual-identity>>.
If present, the `subject:pseudonym` attribute MUST be:

. either a unique identifier selected by the <<certification-authority,CA>> for the <<subject,subject>> of the <<certificate,certificate>>; or
. an identifier verified based on government-issued identity documents.

<<pseudonym,Pseudonym>> <<certificate,certificates>> are not anonymous.
<<certification-authority,CAs>> and <<enterprise-ra,enterprise RAs>> MUST treat <<individual,individual>> identity information relating to a <<pseudonym,pseudonym>> as private in accordance with <<information-treated-as-private>>.

[[uniqueness-of-names]]
==== Uniqueness of names

The <<subject,subjects>> of claim signing <<subscriber,subscribers>> shall be unique.
Name uniqueness is not violated when multiple certificates are issued to the same entity.
For example, A <<certification-authority,CA>> MAY issue certificates with the same <<subject,subject>> `DN` under this <<certificate-policy,CP>> to multiple units of the same organization.
