[[ongoing-subscriber-identification-and-authentication]]
=== Ongoing subscriber identification and authentication

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST re-authenticate the identity of the <<subscriber,subscriber>>, using the procedure described under <<initial-identity-validation>>, no later than:

* 825 days after the last validation of organization identity and authority; and
* 366 days after the last validation of <<individual,individual>> identity, which is the maximum validity period of a <<subscriber,subscriber>> <<certificate,certificate>> (see <<certificate-operational-periods-and-key-pair-usage-periods>>).

These are the same limits that apply to reuse of validation data in <<certificate-application-processing>>.
