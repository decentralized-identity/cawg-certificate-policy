[[private-key-protection-and-cryptographic-module-engineering-controls]]
=== Private key protection and cryptographic module engineering controls

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST implement physical and logical safeguards to prevent unauthorized <<certificate,certificate>> issuance.
Protection of the <<certification-authority,CA>> <<private-key,private key>> outside the validated system or device specified above MUST consist of physical security, encryption, or a combination of both, implemented in a manner that prevents disclosure of the <<private-key,private key>>.
The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST encrypt its <<private-key,private key>> with an algorithm and key-length that, according to the state of the art, are capable of withstanding cryptanalytic attacks for the residual life of the encrypted key or key part.

[[private-key-archival]]
==== Private key archival

Parties other than the <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CA>> MUST NOT archive the <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CA>> <<private-key,private keys>> without authorization by the <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CA>>.

[[private-key-transfer-into-or-from-a-cryptographic-module]]
==== Private key transfer into or from a cryptographic module

If the <<issuing-ca,issuing CA>> generated the <<private-key,private key>> on behalf of the <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CA>>, then the <<issuing-ca,issuing CA>> MUST encrypt the <<private-key,private key>> for transport to the <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CA>>.
If the <<issuing-ca,issuing CA>> becomes aware that a <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CA>>’s <<private-key,private key>> has been communicated to an unauthorized person or an organization not affiliated with the <<subordinate-ca,subordinate CA>>, then the <<issuing-ca,issuing CA>> MUST revoke all <<certificate,certificates>> that include the <<public-key,public key>> corresponding to the communicated <<private-key,private key>>.

[[private-key-storage-on-cryptographic-module]]
==== Private key storage on cryptographic module

The <<certification-authority,CA>> MUST protect its <<private-key,private key>> in a system or device that has been validated as meeting at least FIPS 140-2 level 3, FIPS 140-3 level 3, or an appropriate Common Criteria Protection Profile or Security Target, EAL 4 (or higher), which includes requirements to protect the <<private-key,private key>> and other assets against known threats.
