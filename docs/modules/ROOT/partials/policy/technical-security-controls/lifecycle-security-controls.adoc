[[lifecycle-security-controls]]
=== Lifecycle security controls

. Throughout the certificate lifecycle, from issuance to revocation, security controls MUST be in place to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of certificates and associated data.
. <<certificate,Certificate>> issuance and revocation processes MUST be protected against unauthorized actions and tampering.

. <<certificate,Certificate>> status information MUST be readily available and accessible to relying parties as defined in [.line-through]##certificate status services## and the relevant <<certificate-profile,certificate profiles>>.
